# Stakeholders

## Primary Stakeholders
- **Students:** attendance/performance visibility and recommendations.
- **Faculty:** class operations, alerts, and academic support insights.
- **Placement Officers:** employability tracking and intervention planning.
- **Admin/Registrar:** policy enforcement, reporting, and resource planning.
- **Principal/Management:** KPI dashboards, risk heatmaps, and audit visibility.

## Technical Stakeholders
- **System Admin/Dev Team:** reliability, security, deployment, and monitoring.
- **Data/AI Team:** model quality, explainability, and data governance.

## Governance Stakeholders
- **Compliance/Audit Reviewers:** immutable decision records and traceability.
